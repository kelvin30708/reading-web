texts/diamond-xz.js  — 大般若波羅蜜多經 卷第五百七十七 第九能斷金剛分 (玄奘譯, T0220 / T07n0220 juan 577)
  Text and punctuation from CBETA 電子佛典集成 (Taishō Tripiṭaka electronic version, XML TEI P5):
  https://github.com/cbeta-org/xml-p5/blob/master/T/T07/T07n0220.xml  (juan 577, lines 0980a05–0985c24)
  CBETA header: "Available for non-commercial use when distributed with this header intact."
  Punctuation provided by 眾生出版社 via CBETA. The translation itself (7th c.) is in the public domain.
  The 對話 Dialogue view (speaker turns 世尊 / 善現 / narration) is this app's own arrangement of that text:
  RW_DIALOGUE in the same file (passages: one speaker turn, long ones cut at clause punctuation to fit a phone screen).
  
  The reading-marker titles in the 目錄 are this app's, not traditional 分 titles.

texts/tara-white-praise.js  — 白救度佛母讚 (T1109) with the matching Tibetan སྒྲོལ་དཀར་བསྟོད་པ།
  Chinese: Qing translation by 阿旺扎什, literary work in the public domain.
  E-text prefers Kanripo KR6j0319 (CC BY-SA 4.0): https://www.kanripo.org/text/KR6j0319/001
  Punctuation and the 輪（CBETA）／輸（Taisho）reading follow CBETA T20n1109
  (https://github.com/cbeta-org/xml-p5 ; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — this app is non-commercial;
  share-alike applies to those text files). The Taisho colophon glyph [口*束*頁] / U+210E4 is written as 賴.
  Tibetan: traditionally attributed (in T1109's own colophon) to the 2nd Dalai Lama 根敦嘉措 (1475–1542),
  public domain. Some circulating editions attribute a closely similar praise to the 1st Dalai Lama.
  These are praises (讚) and a longevity mantra, not the full 白度母日修儀軌.

texts/tara-21-praises.js  — 聖救度佛母二十一種禮讚經 (T1108A, 元 安藏) with Derge Kangyur D438 / Toh 438
  Chinese: Yuan translation, public domain. E-text and punctuation from CBETA T20n1108A
  (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; this app is non-commercial; share-alike applies to those text files).
  CBETA readings 禮／災 are used where they correct Taisho 體／炎.
  The imperial preface and the compressed 21-name digest have no Tibetan counterpart in D438.
  The first Anzang quatrain (唵敬禮多哩…) praises the mantra syllables and is not paired with a
  ཕྱག་འཚལ homage verse; the following 21 敬禮 verses are aligned with the 21 Tibetan homage verses.
  Siddham glyphs in the two closing mantras are replaced with the CBETA XML Chinese phonetic lines.
  Tibetan: Esukhia Digital Derge Kangyur (based on the UVA-SOAS 2013 eKangyur), a mechanical
  reproduction of the public-domain Derge woodblock (BDRC W4CZ5369 / Library of Congress),
  itself in the public domain: https://github.com/Esukhia/derge-kangyur
  (archived copy now at OpenPecha/P000001). Credit: Esukhia, UVA-SOAS eKangyur, BDRC W4CZ5369.
  One editorial suggestion {རི་དགས་,རི་དྭགས་} is printed as རི་དྭགས་.
